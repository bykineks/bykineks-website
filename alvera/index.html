Alvera

About

Alvera is a modular Dot Matrix Sans typeface rooted in the raw, pixelated aesthetics of early computing and retro printers. Its forms combine a precise modular structure with distinctive grid-based letterforms, transforming mechanical constraints into a striking typographic identity.

Designed for both functionality and expression, Alvera balances the nostalgia of retro-futurism with modern typographic precision. With 1,060 glyphs spanning Circle and Square dot configurations, Alvera is designed for use across tech interfaces, editorial layouts, esports branding, and experimental digital displays.

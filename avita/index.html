Avita

About

Avita is a geometric sans serif with a tall x-height and sharply cut details. The Hairline lets the geometry of a letter show through, while the Black turns the same shape into a dense headline.

Nine weights come in three widths, each with a matching oblique. Alongside Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters, the family includes celestial symbols, arrows and alternate letterforms.

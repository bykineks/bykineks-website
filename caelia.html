Caelia

Gothic Sans with a sharp architectural character.

About

Caelia is a Gothic Sans typeface shaped by the sharp and monumental character of Gothic architecture. Its forms combine the functional structure of Gothic Sans with pointed geometry, sharp terminals, strong verticality, and sculptural proportions drawn from Gothic architectural details.

The result is a contemporary typeface with a distinctive balance of strength, elegance, and architectural character. Designed for expressive typography, Caelia works across branding, identity, editorial, posters, packaging, and display applications.

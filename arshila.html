About

Arshila is a modern serif typeface shaped by timeless sophistication and refined elegance. Its forms combine classical proportions with high contrast, crisp terminals, and sweeping fluid curves.

With over 2,500 glyphs, Arshila features extensive OpenType capabilities including small caps, petite caps, and stylistic alternates to expand design possibilities. The result is a contemporary typeface with a distinctive balance of strength and delicate beauty.

Designed for expressive typography, Arshila works perfectly across branding, fashion, wedding invitations, luxury skincare packaging, and editorial applications.

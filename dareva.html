Dareva

About

Dareva is a neo-humanist sans serif designed for reading on screen. Micro gives small labels and captions room to breathe. Text holds a steady rhythm across a paragraph. Display brings the same letterforms closer together for headlines.

Each optical size comes in six weights from Extra Light to Bold, with a matching oblique.

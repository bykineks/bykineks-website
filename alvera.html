Alvera

About

Alvera is a modular Dot Matrix Sans typeface rooted in the raw, pixelated aesthetics of early computing and retro printers. Its forms combine a precise modular structure with distinctive grid-based letterforms, transforming mechanical constraints into a striking typographic identity.

Designed for both functionality and expression, Alvera balances the nostalgia of retro-futurism with modern typographic precision. With 1060 characters spanning Circle and Square dot configurations, Alvera is engineered for global usability across tech interfaces, editorial layouts, esports branding, and experimental digital displays.

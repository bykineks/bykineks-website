Bilgen

About

Bilgen is a condensed geometric sans with interlocking capitals. Type two capitals together and their strokes can nest into a single glyph. Try Mix to combine joins from both sets in one word.

Nine weights from Thin to Black come in upright, left-leaning and right-leaning styles.

Kineks Symbol

About

Kineks Symbol is a symbol system designed to work alongside the Kineks family. Its signs give directions on maps, identify facilities in public spaces and mark actions on screen. Arrows and pictograms share proportions so they can sit together on one sign or beside a short label.

Designed by Dede Kurniawan and published by Bykineks in 2026.

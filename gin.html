About

GIN takes its cues from calligraffiti. Try a short word at headline size, then switch between capitals and lowercase to see how the strokes change direction.

Look closely at the letters in “fine flow”: the font has dedicated shapes for the fi and fl pairs. Use the tester to compare them with the individual letters.
